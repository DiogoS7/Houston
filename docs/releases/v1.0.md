# v1.0 – Bench

**Released:** 16 October 2026

The first Houston release covers staffing: who is working on what, how much of their time is billable, and who is heading for the bench.

## What's new

### Power Apps (canvas app)
- **Home** – the consultant's week at a glance: hours logged, timesheet status, team members heading for the bench, a live Power BI tile and in-app notifications
- **My Timesheet** – log hours per engagement and day, billable flag set from the engagement, submit the week for approval
- **Bench Board** – allocation per consultant for the next 6 weeks, coloured green (at target), amber (below target) and red (on the bench)

### Power Automate
- **Timesheet approval** – when a timesheet is submitted, an approval request goes to the consultant's manager; the decision, approver and comment are written back to the timesheet
- **Friday timesheet reminder** – every Friday at 15:00, consultants who haven't submitted get a reminder alert
- **Weekly bench alert** – every Monday at 08:00, managers get an alert for each consultant allocated below target 4 weeks ahead

### Power BI
- **Utilisation** – billable utilisation against target, by week and by consultant
- **Bench forecast** – allocation matrix for the next 8 weeks with the same colour rules as the app
- **Skills** – skill supply (level 3+) against demand from open engagements

### Dataverse
- Shared core data model: Consultants, Clients, Engagements, Skills
- Bench tables: Assignments, Timesheets, Time Entries, Consultant Skills, Engagement Skills
- Alert table for in-app notifications
- Fictional seed data: 12 consultants, 6 clients, 7 engagements, 6 weeks of timesheets

### Engineering
- Solution source unpacked in `solution/` with the Power Platform CLI
- Power BI report stored as a Power BI Project (`.pbip`) with a custom Houston theme
- GitHub Actions CI validates the seed data and packs the solution on every push

## Known limitations
- The demo tenant has no mailboxes, so notifications are in-app alerts and every approval is assigned to one demo account (it carries "on behalf of" the real manager).
- People & Skills screen moves to v1.1 SkillPath.
